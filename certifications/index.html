Healthcare

NHA CPCT-A
Certified Patient Care Technician/Assistant
Patient Care Technicians provide hands-on assistance to serve patients' basic needs, which greatly impacts the lives of their patients and their patients' families.
AHA CPR/BLS
Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation/Basic Life Support
The AHAs BLS course trains participants to promptly recognize several life-threatening emergencies, give high-quality chest compressions, deliver appropriate ventilations and provide early use of an AED.

CyberSecurity

GIAC GSEC
Security Essentials
The GIAC Security Essentials (GSEC) certification validates a practitioner's knowledge of information security beyond simple terminology and concepts. GSEC certification holders are demonstrating that they are qualified for hands-on IT systems roles with respect to security tasks.
GIAC GFACT
Foundational Cybersecurity Technologies
GIAC Foundational Cybersecurity Technologies (GFACT) certification validates a practitioner's knowledge of essential foundational cybersecurity concepts.

Computer Science

Unity Certified Associate: Game Developer

This certification is designed for future game developers who want to showcase their mastery of core Unity skills and concepts to obtain their first professional Unity role.
