12 am - 3 am.

To most, this time frame is one of relaxation, sleep, and calm. To me, this time frame is one of hard work, determination, and connection with myself. To some, this time frame is one of isolation - which they fear. To me, this time frame of isolation - is when I thrive most.

In this short time frame of the day - I work to the absolute best of what I can do, yet I always need more. I seem to be the most calm, yet also the most worked up. I am the most productive at this time, yet I always have something to do the next day. I get the least sleep of my friends, yet I am the most active.

Whenever I talk with someone about the time at which I normally do my homework or work on personal projects, they say that I am "a sweat" or "tryhard." However, my close friends know the true reason why - I try to stretch out the hours in a day and try to become a better version of myself with every midnight work session. Do not just take my word for it, hear what they have to say.

Ask Brady, he would tell you "Working on yet another coding project ."

Ask Hudson, he would say "Another cybersecurity capture-the-flag challenge."

Ask Alana, she would say "Taking a peaceful walk in the night."

What if I told you that while they are all correct, they couldn't be farther from the truth? Is procrastination the reason I am up this late - no. Is it stubbornness - possibly. During this period of the day, my brain seems to be able to grasp some of the most difficult concepts in some of the most simple of manners.

Sometime shortly after midnight - September 4, 2023: everything just clicked. I was studying for my first cybersecurity certification and was reading my textbook on how files are stored in Linux - such as the /shadow/ and /etc/ folders. At this moment, the entire course just merged into one thought. With this epiphany, I started digging into the various commands used for "moving" around the files. Little did I know, these commands proved valuable in achieving my certification that day.

12:39 am - December 2, 2023: I created the first update to my personal website. Little did I know at that time, I had begun a journey that would lead to many late nights, learning how websites are developed and incrementally improving my own. Many of the "coding sessions" I have had/continue to have regarding my website happen after midnight, as my brain has no trouble understanding the complex logic and solving the problems of trying to perfect a website from scratch.

During the summer between my junior and senior year, I participated in a dual enrollment class at the Georgia Insitute of Technology. In said class, we had problem sets - with various styles of programming questions developed to test our computational thinking skills. One such question was the Unit 5 -> 5.1 Problem Set -> Question 15. At 1 pm, I had tried many different solutions - to no avail. This question had me stumped. After 45 minutes of straight debugging for this one question, I decided to put it off until the evening. At 1:04 am that night, I returned to Question 15. I cleared my previous answer, took a deep breath, and re-read it. Instantly, a thought popped into my head. I typed out the code - and crossed my fingers. Miraculously, my code had worked for a question that had stumped me just 12 hours earlier.

Arguably the best - and my favorite - part of this essay, is that it was started, written, revised, and completed at 2 am.
